Local SEO

Get found by people searching near you.

Get a free quote

What it is

Local SEO helps nearby customers find your business in Google Maps and local results. It matters most for shops, clinics, restaurants and service businesses.

Who it is for

Businesses with a physical location or a defined service area.

What is included

  • Google Business Profile setup and optimisation
  • Local citations and directories
  • Review management
  • Local landing pages
  • NAP consistency (name, address, phone)
